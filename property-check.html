How do we access the home without key custody?

Agree access before booking: provide a temporary smart-lock or key-safe code, arrange an authorised key handover through the development concierge, or have you, a family member or tenant open the door. If we need to collect or return a key at a different address, a €15–€20 travel supplement may apply and will be agreed in advance.

A visual check, not a technical survey

The visit records visible conditions and is not a technical inspection or professional diagnosis. Any travel supplement or third-party work is confirmed with you before booking.
