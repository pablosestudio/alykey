RENOVATIONS & REPAIRS · ALICANTE

Repair and renovation coordination in Alicante for owners living abroad

Need work done at your property but cannot be there? ALYKEY finds tradespeople, gathers quotes for you to review, and coordinates access and on-site follow-up. You decide what to approve and when work can begin.

One point of contact to organise work at your property

We look for suitable tradespeople for the requested job, check availability and request quotes, then share the information received so you can compare. Once you approve an option, we coordinate dates, property access and communication during the work.

We can help organise plumbing, electrical, carpentry, painting, masonry and small renovation work, subject to availability and project scope.

HOW IT WORKS

You approve. We coordinate on site.

1. Tell us about the work

Explain the issue or improvement. If needed, we arrange a visit to understand the scope.

2. Review your options

We contact tradespeople and share the availability and quotes they provide.

3. Approve before work starts

No work is commissioned and no extra cost or change is accepted without your approval.

4. We coordinate and update you

We arrange access and dates, provide agreed on-site follow-up and share updates and photos.

A one-off service, with no monthly plan required

You can request coordination for a specific job without subscribing to property care. Keyholding, routine visits and trades coordination are separate services; the scope and cost of each assignment are confirmed in advance.

Finding tradespeople, requesting and comparing quotes, and coordinating a renovation are not automatically charged at €30 per hour. Once we understand the scope, we will send a separate coordination proposal covering the trades involved, the work required and the agreed follow-up. You will know the fee before accepting, and we will agree any extra scope with you before it creates an additional charge. The €30-per-hour Personal Concierge rate applies to straightforward errands and is a separate service.

Who contracts and carries out the repair?

Tradespeople are independent and responsible for assessing and carrying out their specialist work. The owner reviews and contracts the work directly with the tradesperson and pays their invoices directly. ALYKEY separately provides and invoices the agreed search and coordination service.

ALYKEY’s follow-up is operational: scheduling, access, communication and visual progress checks as agreed. It does not replace technical project direction, a technical design, certifications or statutory inspections, which must be provided by qualified professionals when required.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a monthly plan?

No. You can request one-off coordination without keyholding or routine visits.

Who chooses the tradesperson and approves the quote?

You choose and approve the quote before work is commissioned. You also approve any changes or extra costs in advance.

Does ALYKEY carry out repairs?

No. ALYKEY finds tradespeople and coordinates the job; each independent professional assesses and carries out their specialist work.

What does follow-up include?

Agreed operational on-site follow-up, access and schedule coordination, communication and photo updates. It is not technical direction or certification.

How much does it cost?

You receive a coordination proposal based on the scope before confirming the service. Tradespeople’s work is quoted and invoiced separately. We agree any expanded scope with you before additional costs are incurred.
