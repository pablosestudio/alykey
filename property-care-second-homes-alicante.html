Visual visits so you know how your home is doing

A property left empty for weeks may need attention while its owner is far away. ALYKEY offers scheduled visual visits, keyholding for one authorised set of keys, and photo updates.

Visits look at visible features such as doors, windows and apparent signs of leaks or damp. They are not technical surveys and cannot guarantee that hidden faults will be found.

Visit plans while you are away

Choose a visit frequency based on how long your home is usually empty. Each scheduled visit is 30–45 minutes.

A one-off check if you do not need a monthly plan

The Property Check includes one visit of up to 45 minutes, a visual checklist, up to 15 photos and a written report within 24 hours. It costs €39; the short-video option costs €45 in total.

It is a practical starting point or a way to check the home before or after a period away, without recurring visits. It is not a survey or diagnosis by a qualified tradesperson.

If we spot an issue, we tell you before arranging work

If we notice an apparent issue during a visit, we send you the information available so you can decide what to do. We can coordinate access for an independent professional, request a quote and keep you informed.

We do not book chargeable work or third-party purchases without your approval. The professional's labour and materials are quoted separately.

Access coordination for independent professionals

When a booked technician or provider needs to enter the home, ALYKEY can arrange access for €25 for up to 60 minutes. Any extra time or coordination fee is quoted and approved before booking.

Plumbing, electrical, air-conditioning, cleaning, gardening and pool-care work is performed by independent professionals. ALYKEY coordinates access and communication; specialist trade work is not represented as work carried out by ALYKEY's own team.

Get the home ready before you arrive

You can also ask us to arrange agreed tasks to prepare the home, receive a delivery or meet a booked professional before you arrive.

Concierge assistance costs €30 per hour, with a one-hour minimum. We agree how any additional time will be billed before booking. Purchases and third-party services are charged separately, with your approval in advance.

Frequently asked questions about second-home care

What is included in a monthly visit?

One scheduled visual visit of 30–45 minutes. Every included visit comes with a digital report and photos within 24 hours.

Does ALYKEY carry out repairs?

Specialist work is performed by independent professionals. ALYKEY can coordinate access and communication, and shares costs for your approval before booking.

How much does second-home care in Alicante cost?

Published monthly plans cost €49 for one visit, €89 for two and €159 for four. The one-off Property Check is €39, or €45 with a short video. We confirm the scope and total before booking.

Do the prices cover homes outside Alicante city?

Published prices apply to standard homes in Alicante city. For other areas, we check availability and confirm any travel charge before booking.

Will a visual visit find every fault?

No. It is not a technical survey and cannot guarantee that hidden faults will be found.

Can I book only one visit?

Yes. Property Check is a one-off service with a written report and an optional short video; it does not require a monthly plan.
