How do we enter if you do not have key custody with ALYKEY?

For a Property Check, arrange access in advance through a smart lock or key safe code, an authorised handover at your development's concierge, or by having you, a family member or tenant open the door. We will confirm the access method before booking. If a key must be collected or returned at another address, a €15–€20 travel supplement may apply and will be agreed beforehand.

What each price covers

Prices are indicative totals, including applicable taxes, for standard homes in Alicante city. We confirm availability, service scope, any travel charge and the final price in writing before booking. Independent professionals set their own fees; their labour and materials are separate. No paid extra is arranged without your approval.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers before you book

When will I receive the report?

For every visit included in Essential, Plus or Weekly, we send a digital report with photos within 24 hours. Property Check reports are also delivered within 24 hours. Custodia Base has no scheduled inspection visits, so it does not include periodic visit reports.

Can you check my property if ALYKEY does not hold my keys?

Yes. Before a Property Check, we agree a safe access method: a temporary smart-lock code, a key safe, an authorised key handover through the development concierge, or an owner, relative or tenant letting us in. We do not enter until access has been agreed.

What is the difference between four visits per month and weekly cover?

The Weekly plan at €159 includes four visits per month, or 48 visits per year. If you need a visit in every one of the 52 weeks, the continuous-cover option is €172 per month.

Are repairs and materials included in the plans?

No. The plans cover the listed visits and visual checks. Repairs, specialist work, materials and third-party services are quoted separately and only arranged after you approve them.

Is a home check a technical inspection?

No. Checks are visual and report what can be seen during the visit. They are not a technical survey or a professional diagnosis and cannot guarantee detection of hidden defects.

How do I start a plan or request a service?

Use the button on the plan or service you want and send us your details. We will confirm availability, access arrangements, scope and price before the service begins. A request through this website is not an online payment.
